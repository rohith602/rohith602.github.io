Hummingbird: a nectar-collecting agent

Trained an agent to fly through a 3D environment and feed from flowers using Proximal Policy Optimisation, then took the training run apart to find out why it was slow.

  • Found two reasons the first run stopped at 41.29: it ran out of steps while its learning rate decayed to zero, and it saw one frame at a time, so it could not sense its own velocity. Stacking three frames and a ten times larger step budget raised mean reward to 96.58.
  • Profiled the run and found 95.5% of wall-clock time was Unity physics on the CPU, so a bigger GPU would have bought almost nothing.
  • Benchmarked process layouts on a 28-vCPU cloud instance: splitting 28 arenas across 7 processes instead of 1 tripled throughput.
  • Exported the trained policy to ONNX. It runs inside Unity with no Python connection, in the Editor and on an Android phone.
Built withUnity 6, ML-Agents 4.1, PPO, Python, ONNX, Android

Four decades of video game sales

A Flask web app that analyses 16,598 video game sales records and draws ten Matplotlib charts from them: where games sell, and how that has changed since 1980.

  • Ten charts covering sales over time, genre share by year, regional sales by genre, and North America against Europe correlation.
  • Dropped rows with no release year and cut the time series at 2016, where the dataset's coverage thins out.
  • North America accounts for 49% of all units sold, more than Europe and Japan combined.
Built withPython, pandas, Matplotlib, Flask

Hospital emergency room dashboard

A four-page Power BI report over 9,216 emergency room visits from April 2023 to October 2024, covering patient volume, waiting times, satisfaction and referrals.

  • Four headline measures (patients, average wait, satisfaction, referrals), each shown as a monthly trend and as a consolidated view.
  • Breaks patients down by age group, gender, race, department referral and admission status, plus a day-of-week by wait-time matrix for staffing.
  • DAX measures over the visit table and a separate date table, so the year and month slicers filter every visual together.
  • Findings page: average wait 35.3 minutes, satisfaction 4.99 out of 10, and Saturday the busiest day with 1,377 patients.
Built withPower BI, DAX, data modelling
