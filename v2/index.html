01 / Reinforcement Learning

Hummingbird — a nectar-collecting agent

Unity 6 ML-Agents 4.1 PPO Python ONNX

Trained an agent to fly through a 3D environment and feed from flowers using Proximal Policy Optimisation, then took the training run apart to find out why it was slow.

  • Diagnosed a reward plateau as an observation-space limitation, not a hyperparameter one — a force-controlled body cannot be steered without a sense of its own velocity.
  • Profiled the run and found 95.5% of wall-clock time was Unity physics on the CPU, so a bigger GPU would have bought almost nothing.
  • Benchmarked process layouts on a 28-vCPU cloud instance: splitting 28 arenas across 7 processes instead of 1 tripled throughput.
  • Exported the trained policy to ONNX and ran it back inside the Unity editor.

02 / Data Analysis

Four decades of video game sales

Python pandas Matplotlib Jupyter

A visualisation project over 16,500+ sales records, built to answer a simple question properly: where does a game actually sell, and has that changed?

  • Ten charts covering regional market share, cross-region sales correlation, and genre popularity over time.
  • Cleaned inconsistent release years and missing publisher fields before any of the analysis was trustworthy.
  • Found the regional split is far less even than the headline "global sales" number suggests.

03 / Business Intelligence

Hospital emergency room dashboard

Power BI DAX Excel Data modelling

A four-page interactive dashboard over roughly 9,200 emergency room patient records, built so a hospital administrator could find a staffing gap without asking an analyst.

  • Tracks average wait time, admission rate and patient satisfaction as headline measures.
  • Every measure breaks down by department referral, age group, gender and time of day.
  • Written as DAX measures over a modelled table rather than pre-aggregated sheets, so the filters actually compose.
